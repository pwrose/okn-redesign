Crosswalks

Shared identifiers and mappings that link the OKN knowledge graphs

Verified crosswalks connect knowledge graphs that were built independently, letting a single question span them. Explore them as a network, or look them up in the full inventory.

Crosswalk network Interactive map of which knowledge graphs connect to which, and how densely. Hover an edge for detail, filter by domain or join method. Interactive visualization Crosswalk inventory The full table of every crosswalk, grouped by domain, with the shared identifier and example research questions for each.
